About

Small apps, made with care.

Velkmoor is a small, independent studio. We build apps for the moments when people are stuck, and we measure each one by a simple question: is the person using it glad they had it?

That's why our apps ask for no account, keep your data on your device, and keep the core help free. They are tools, not treatments, and they never stand in for a doctor or pharmacist.
