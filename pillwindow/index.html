Questions

Does Pill Window tell me how long to wait?

No. You set the wait to match your prescription label, from 10 to 180 minutes. Pill Window doesn't give medical advice or dosing guidance.

What if I forget to tap?

If you took it a few minutes ago, move the start time back. If you forgot entirely, add a missed log afterwards, up to 30 days back.

Do I need notifications on?

For the alert when your wait is up, yes. Without notifications, Pill Window can't let you know the moment the wait ends.

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads. It works offline.

What's free, and what does Plus add?

The timer, alerts, Lock Screen countdown, morning reminder and your last 30 days are free. Plus adds your full history, patterns and CSV export.

Pill Window is a timer and a log. It does not provide medical advice, dosing guidance or treatment recommendations. Always follow your prescription label and your clinician's instructions.
