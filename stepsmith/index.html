Stepsmith

Big tasks, in tiny steps.

Stuck before you start? Stepsmith turns a big task into small, concrete steps, then shows you one at a time. Type it in one line. Tap Done, and the next one appears.

Coming soon on the App Store iPhone & iPad · iOS 26 · Free to start

Start here

Type it in one line.

“Clean my room.” “Reply to the landlord.” “Pay the electric bill.” That's enough to begin.

  • Get 3–8 small steps, each starting with an action.
  • Edit, reorder, add or delete any of them.
  • Paste a list and each line becomes a step.

One thing at a time

One step on screen.

Tap Start, and there's only the step in front of you. Done. Next. Nothing else to see.

  • Your first task is broken down during setup, and you finish its first step before anything else.

When it's time

A gentle nudge to start.

Set a start time for any task: once, every day or on weekdays. The reminder shows your next step.

  • Done, 10 min later and Open, right in the notification.
  • An optional check-in for untouched tasks, up to three a day, never during your quiet hours.

Every day

Daily basics, already split.

Taking out the trash, a shower, laundry, dishes, a simple meal, bedtime and more, each already split into two to four small steps.

  • Turn on the ones you want. Today's list resets every morning.
  • No streaks to break.

Made on your device.
Stepsmith doesn't send what you type.

On-device AI

Steps are written by Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence). Nothing you type is sent to us or to any server.

Works without it, too

If your device can't run Apple Intelligence, Stepsmith says so plainly and uses its built-in splitter instead.

Your lists are yours

No account, no ads, works offline. Export everything as a file anytime, free.

Questions

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

Only for AI breakdowns. Without it, Stepsmith uses its built-in splitter, and you can always write or paste your own steps. Stepsmith requires iOS 26.

Is what I type sent anywhere?

No. Breakdowns are made on your device, and your lists are saved on your device. There's no account.

What's free, and what does Plus add?

You can write or paste unlimited breakdowns, use the focus screen, reminders and daily basics, and get 3 AI breakdowns a month for free. Plus adds unlimited AI breakdowns, “Make this step smaller”, step timers, unlimited routines and history search.

Is Stepsmith a medical app?

No. Stepsmith is a planning tool for anyone who gets stuck before starting a task. It is not a medical device, and it doesn't diagnose or treat ADHD or any other condition.

Can I move my lists to a new device?

Yes. Export everything as a file, then import it on the new device. Import merges and never deletes what you have.
