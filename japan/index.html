Sample: our own app, English to Japanese

We are a new service and have no client work to show yet. This sample is Stepsmith, an iOS app we build ourselves. Keyword data was checked on the Japanese App Store on 15 October 2026.

FieldEnglish (US)Japanese, with back-translation
NameStepsmith: Task BreakdownStepsmith：タスク分解と先延ばし対策
“Task breakdown and beating procrastination.” Both terms are searched in Japan, and none of the top 10 results for either had more than 5,000 ratings.
SubtitleTask Paralysis? Start Small後回しを、今できる小さな一歩に
“Turn what you keep putting off into one small step you can do now.” “Task paralysis” has no natural Japanese term; “putting off” is what people type.
Keywordsadhd, starter, unstuck…Single words that combine with the name, such as 管理 (“management”) to form タスク管理 (“task management”). Sensitive terms stay in the keyword field, never in visible copy.
First lineStuck before you start?始める前に、手が止まってしまう。
“Before I start, my hands just stop.” Plain and polite: Japanese store copy that shouts reads cheap.

A real finding from our own string review. Japanese has no spaces, so the line broke inside the verb 持っていく (“carry”). The fix is a shorter string or a break point between phrases. A translation file alone never shows this.

Questions

Will this get my app ranked in Japan?

We can't promise rankings, downloads or revenue, and nobody honestly can. We choose terms where a new app can realistically appear, and explain the trade-offs. Keyword data is a snapshot of the day we checked.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for first drafts and checks. Every line is written and approved by a native Japanese speaker, and keywords come from the Japanese store itself, not from translating your English keywords.

Do you need my App Store Connect login?

No. You get paste-ready text. If you prefer, you can add us as a user with a limited role.

Are my strings and unreleased features kept private?

Yes. We never share your files or use them to train anything, and we delete them within 30 days of delivery, or sooner if you ask. We are happy to sign your NDA.

What won't you write?

Competitor names in your keywords, medical or financial claims, and anything App Review is likely to reject. We will tell you if your English listing has one of these too.

Who owns the translation?

You do, once paid. Use it anywhere for your app.
