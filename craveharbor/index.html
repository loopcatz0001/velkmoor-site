Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for or log in to.

What happens if I slip?

A slip is logged, never a reset. Your quit date and your best streak stay, and your estimates are adjusted for it.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, protected by its built-in data protection. It's included in your own iCloud or computer backups, and you can export a backup file anytime. It is never sent to us.

What's free, and what does Plus add?

Craving logging and craving help are always free, along with your quit counter, savings goal, cut-down plan, widgets and backups. Plus adds a plan for every trigger, the full trigger ranking, peak craving hours and 30-day trends.

Is Crave Harbor a medical treatment?

No. It's a tracking and self-help tool. For support with quitting, talk to a doctor or call 1-800-QUIT-NOW.
